# Confirmed Commercial Rules

## Paid product
Adeni is paid B2B SaaS. There is no permanent free tier.

## Plans
- Starter
- Professional
- Business

Product philosophy:
> Starter lets a wellness business operate.
> Professional helps the business grow and automate.
> Business helps the organization scale and control teams/locations.

Professional should be marked **Most Popular**.

## Launch monthly pricing

### Canada
- Starter: CAD 99
- Professional: CAD 149
- Business: CAD 249

### Nigeria
- Starter: NGN 35,000
- Professional: NGN 55,000
- Business: NGN 95,000

### United States
US is not enabled for self-serve launch yet. Architecture must support future US pricing.

## Country pricing, not city pricing
Pricing is by billing country, never discovery city/market.

## GMV platform fee at launch
- Canada: 0%
- Nigeria: 0%

The model must support future per-country/per-plan GMV fees without migration.

## Customer checkout
Customer pays only the business-defined amount.
A future Adeni platform fee is business-paid and deducted from merchant economics/settlement, not added to the customer total.

## Non-custodial
Adeni should not hold customer funds or operate a stored-value wallet.

## Public pricing
Discover should expose transparent self-serve plan pricing on `/plan` or equivalent.
