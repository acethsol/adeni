How Adeni works for your customers

When you register, you are building the listing customers see on Discover — categories first, then bookable services.

Customer journey

  1. Pick a market — Lagos, Ottawa, or another city. Discovery and search use this context.
  2. Explore a category — e.g. Massage, Hair & Grooming, Nails. Same taxonomy in every market; featured order can differ.
  3. Find your business — You appear when your primary or any additional category matches the filter.
  4. Book a service — Customer picks a service you added (from the catalog or custom), chooses a slot, and confirms.

Your setup (business portal)

  1. Primary category — Main type of business (used for SEO and default sorting).
  2. Additional categories — Optional tags (spa also offering massage + nails).
  3. Services — Add from Adeni templates or create your own; each ties to a category and booking type (appointment, class, session, …).
  4. Availability — Weekly hours so customers can book online.
